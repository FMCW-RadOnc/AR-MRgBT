- rolling_interval
    * For the "Rolling Average" and "Rolling Median" filter, how many samples to take into account
- kf_process_noise_coef_mm
    * For the "Kalman Filter" filter, what process noise (in mm) to assume
- kf_observation_noise_coef_mm
    * For the "Kalman Filter" filter, what observation noise (in mm) to assume
- one_euro_min_cutoff_hz
    * For the "One Euro Filter", the base cutoff frequency. Lower values suppress more stationary jitter but add more lag to slow movement
- one_euro_beta
    * For the "One Euro Filter", how strongly the cutoff rises with movement speed. Higher values follow deliberate movement faster but allow more noise through
- one_euro_derivative_cutoff_hz
    * For the "One Euro Filter", the cutoff frequency used to smooth the speed estimate
- one_euro_max_speed_mm_s
    * For the "One Euro Filter", the maximum physically plausible coil speed. Faster one-sample jumps are treated as tracking outliers
- target_update_freq_ms
    * How often to update the list of possible targets (in ms) 
- display_update_freq_ms
    * How often to update the needle display (in ms). Every tracking position received during this window contributes to the coordinate-wise median shown at the end of the window.
- green_threshold_mm
    * The maximum possible distance from the target (in mm) for the axis display to be green
- yellow_threshold_mm
    * The maximum possible distance from the target (in mm) for the axis display to be yellow
- max_threshold_mm
    * The minimum distance from the target (in mm) to fully fill the axis display.
- dist_between_rx1_and_needle_tip_mm
    * The physical distance between the RX1 core and the tip of the needle (in mm)

Pullback settings (the nested "pullback" object, separate from display filters):
- expected_coil_separation_mm (null), coil_separation_tolerance_mm (2), calibration_samples (7)
    * Validate rigid RX1/RX2 spacing during capture. Null learns the median of the first seven otherwise valid frames; bootstrap readings are rechecked on Stop. A known physical baseline can be configured.
- neighbors_each_side (3), max_neighbor_time_distance_s (1.5)
    * Candidate-excluded local Z reference: up to three readings on each side within 1.5 seconds. References cannot cross a needle-switch acquisition block.
- z_outlier_floor_mm (5), z_outlier_sigma (4.5), minimum_supporting_neighbors (4)
    * Robust nonincreasing Z/time model. Threshold = max(5 mm, median reference residual + 4.5 * 1.4826 * median absolute deviation of reference residuals). Reject the entire candidate row only when four reference neighbors support the model and the candidate exceeds its threshold. Insufficient reference retains the original row for reversal repair.
- reversal_radius_rows (3), reversal_radius_seconds (1.5)
    * Repair Z increases using bounded nearby rows. Prefer the most original rows, then lower local-model error, then time coverage. No distant fixed future boundary and no unbounded expansion. Resolved windows are protected. Z strictly decreases globally per needle; new acquisition blocks cannot erase earlier blocks.
- neighbor_agreement_mm (2), minimum_spike_excursion_mm (3)
    * Check X and Y separately after Z selection. Immediate readings before/after an excursion must agree within 2 mm; each candidate must be more than 3 mm beyond both in the same direction. Whole rows are rejected if either axis qualifies.
- reference_rows_per_side (3), side_reference_tolerance_mm (2), maximum_spike_run (2)
    * Test isolated one- or two-row excursions. Separate medians of up to three rows on each side must agree within neighbor_agreement_mm; candidates must be more than 2 mm beyond both medians. Different side levels protect sustained transitions. Singleton spikes cannot support rejecting a neighboring block. Decisions use the same unchanged Z-selected coordinates.
- gross_axis_step_mm (10), maximum_run_gap_seconds (1), minimum_coherent_run (3)
    * Split X/Y reference runs at a step over 10 mm in either axis or gap over 1 second. Reject runs shorter than three rows. Endpoints of supported runs stay original.
- max_gap_seconds (0.5)
    * Internal diagnostic section threshold in the recorder. This does not break the drawn contour: MIM extension 2.10.1 connects five-column exports across timestamp gaps. Older marked CSVs still retain their explicit sections.

Exports contain only Needle, Timestamp and tracker XYZ, with padded spacing. Coordinates and timestamps remain original; there is no grouping, averaging, fitted endpoint or resampling. MIM extension 2.10.1 draws through final anchors without a second rejection pass. The old fixed 2 mm Z consensus, global decreasing-path search, axial speed limit, and depth-based straight-line X/Y check have been replaced.

All numeric pullback settings must be positive and finite. Count settings must be integers. Defaults reproduce the reviewed comparisons; they do not establish alignment to the image or identify the sheath exit. Start and stop capture at the intended withdrawal interval.
